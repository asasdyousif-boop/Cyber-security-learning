Product Cipher

Description

A Product Cipher combines two or more encryption techniques, commonly substitution and transposition.

Encryption

1. Apply a substitution operation to the plaintext.
2. Apply a transposition operation to the result.
3. The final output is the ciphertext.

Decryption

1. Reverse the transposition operation.
2. Reverse the substitution operation.
3. Recover the original plaintext.
